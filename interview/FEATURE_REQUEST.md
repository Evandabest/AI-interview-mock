# Feature request #1043: Auction search and filters

**Status:** Ready for development  
**Category:** Auction discovery  
**Priority:** Medium

## Customer feedback

> I'm looking for a keyboard within my budget, preferably one whose auction ends soon. It's difficult to narrow down the listings, and when I refresh or share the page, my search choices disappear.

## Problem statement

Improve the auction browsing experience so customers can find items that interest them without scrolling through every listing.

The page already has basic search and filter controls. Review the current experience and build on it where needed.

## Requested improvements

- Help customers search for auctions by title.
- Let customers narrow results by price and how soon an auction ends.
- Make search and filters useful together.
- Allow customers to return to or share their search.
- Provide useful feedback when a search has no results or something needs attention.

## Delivery

Investigate the existing behavior, decide on a reasonable scope, and implement the improvements. Explain your assumptions, any tradeoffs, and how you verified the experience. Add sample listings if needed to demonstrate your work.
